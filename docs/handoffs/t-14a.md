     - `grabFrames(videoFile: Blob | File, frameCount?: number, options?: GrabFramesOptions): Promise<Blob[]>`:
       Samples 1 to 4 frames (default 4) from browser HTMLVideoElement. Computes deterministic interior sample times `duration * (i + 0.5) / count` to avoid zero-seek and end-of-stream freeze bugs. Sequentially seeks and captures canvas frames as JPEG Blobs with white background, bounded to 1280px width.
