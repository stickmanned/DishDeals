05 — Voice and imagery

Short, warm, specific.

Voice

Like a friend who knows the neighbourhood. Name the dish, the price and the time. Skip superlatives.

“Good evening. Sushi deals near you.”
Not: “Unbeatable offers await!”
Photography

Natural light, 45° overhead or table-level, dark wood and stone surfaces, one hero dish per frame. No stock-style smiling groups, no heavy filters.

Deals

Offers always use the saffron tint with DM Mono, never red. Red is reserved for actions and places, so a deal never competes with a button.
