Do
Float glass above content: search, filters, tab bar, pins, sheet headers.
Keep a lit top-left edge and a faint bottom-right edge on every pane.
Keep primary controls at least 48 pt tall.
Nest at most one glass layer inside another.
Don't
Put prices, conditions or long copy on glass.
Stack more than two glass layers on screen at once.
Use refraction on small chips (the filter distorts text).
Use glass fills below .40 white behind text.
